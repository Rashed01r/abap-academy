Contact page: pick a design

Three mockups in the ABAP Academy dark theme, each with email, Saudi mobile, LinkedIn and GitHub. Each one works: try the form, the validation and the mobile size (narrow the window).

A · Split panelYour details on the left, the form on the right. Stacks on mobile. B · Centered formOne centered column: email and phone chips, then the form. The most minimal. C · Contact cardsFour clickable cards (Email, Mobile, LinkedIn, GitHub) with the form below. The most visual.

Spam protection in all three

  • Your email and number are not in the page source; they are put together when the page runs, so simple scraper bots cannot harvest them.
  • A hidden trap field that only bots fill in. Bots that fill it get a fake "sent" message.
  • Messages sent less than 3 seconds after the page opened are treated as bots.
  • At most two links per message, strict length limits, and one message per minute from the same browser.
  • User text is shown with plain text only, never as HTML, so nothing typed can run as code.

These checks run in the browser. The final page adds the protection of whichever delivery method you choose: opening the visitor's email app (nothing for bots to post to) or a form service with captcha.
